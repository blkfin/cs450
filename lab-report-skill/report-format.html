Lab Report Guideline (target format)

Your skill must produce a report in exactly this format. Use the checklist at the bottom to test its output.

Required sections, in this order

  1. Title block: lab number and name, date, and the line "Prepared from lab notebook."
  2. Objective: one or two sentences on what the lab set out to find.
  3. Materials and Methods: chemicals with concentrations, equipment, and the steps followed. Past tense, written as a paragraph, not a list.
  4. Data: one table with columns Trial, Start (mL), End (mL), Volume Used (mL), Observation. Every trial appears, including failed ones.
  5. Results: the calculation shown step by step, with units on every number, and the final answer stated plainly.
  6. Discussion: which trials were used and why, any trial that was excluded and why, and at least one source of error.
  7. Uncertain Data: every value the notes were unsure about, marked [UNCERTAIN], with what the notes actually said. Write "None" if there are none.

Formatting rules

  • Markdown. Each section starts with a ## heading using the exact names above.
  • Copy numbers exactly from the notes. Never invent or "fix" a measurement.
  • If the notes contain a mistake that was corrected, report only the corrected result.
  • Formal, third person voice. No "I", no slang, no question marks.
  • No names of partners or students in the report.
  • Units on every number (mL, L, M, mol).

Example paragraph (from a different lab)

Materials and Methods, Density of an Unknown Metal: A sample of unknown metal was massed on an electronic balance to 0.01 g. A 50 mL graduated cylinder was filled with water to 25.0 mL, and the sample was lowered in carefully to avoid splashing. The new water level was recorded, and the displaced volume was taken as the volume of the sample. The procedure was repeated for three trials.

Checklist for testing your skill

  • All seven sections present, in order, with exact headings
  • Data table includes every trial
  • Results show the calculation with units, and only the corrected answer
  • Discussion explains any excluded trial
  • Uncertain values are flagged, not invented
  • No first person, no names
