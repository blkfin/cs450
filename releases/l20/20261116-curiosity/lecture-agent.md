## Deep inquiry points four questions at your project topic.

- Source lineage: `cs450-nov16-curiosity#sections.four_directions`
- Citations: `hw_faculty_curiosity`

| Direction | Looks for | Asks about your project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | First principles: the core idea | What are the fundamental concepts? |
| East | Related concepts: the connections | What are the parallels in other fields? |
| South | Application: the real world | How does it apply in real-life situations? |
| West | History and origin: the context | How did this idea evolve? |

## Your group splits the compass, one direction per person.

- Source lineage: `cs450-nov16-curiosity#sections.split_one_direction_each`
- Citations: `hw_faculty_curiosity`

- You know the four directions
- Alone, you would skim four answers
- Split them: one direction each, all at once

### One project topic, split four ways

| Member | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runs | North | East | South | West |

- **Accurate?**: Calling a claim wrong needs contrary evidence. An unfamiliar claim can stay unverified.
- **Deep enough?**: Look for what is absent: an omission, not an error.

## Run your one direction on your project topic.

- Source lineage: `cs450-nov16-curiosity#sections.run_your_direction`
- Citations: `hw_faculty_curiosity`

Replace [your topic] with your project topic. Run only your direction and take notes.

- NORTH: What are the first principles and core ideas behind [your topic]?
- EAST: What other fields or concepts connect to [your topic]?
- SOUTH: Create three real-world scenarios where [your topic] is applied. What works well? What are the problems?
- WEST: Explain the origin and history of [your topic].

**Response mode:** written

**Time:** 600 seconds

## On your own: what did the AI miss in your direction?

- Source lineage: `cs450-nov16-curiosity#sections.solo_pause`
- Citations: `hw_faculty_curiosity`

- You each hold one judged answer from one direction
- A smooth answer is not always a complete one

- **Gap**: What an answer left out, misread, or got wrong.

Write your own judgment before comparing it with the group's. No talking, no peeking.

- What did the AI get wrong or oversimplify in your direction?
- What one question needs investigating?

**Response mode:** written

**Time:** 180 seconds
