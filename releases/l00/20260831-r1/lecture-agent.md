## CS 450: AI and the World

- Source lineage: `cs450-syllabus-brief#sections.course`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Fall 2026 · 4 credits · Computer Science

Discovery Program, Environment, Technology, and Society (ETS). Not writing intensive.

- **What**: Nontechnical introduction to modern AI: machine learning, neural networks, and generative models.
- **How**: Modern AI systems generate outputs probabilistically and can produce convincing errors.
- **Then**: Apply AI to your own field, including implications and ethical considerations.

## Nine topics move from how AI works to responsible use in your field.

- Source lineage: `cs450-syllabus-brief#sections.topics`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### How AI works and is used

- How AI systems learn
- How generative AI produces responses
- Models, products, context, and continuity
- Prompting, context, and reliable information
- AI agents, tools, and uses in your field

### Testing AI and understanding its effects

- Testing AI systems and finding their limits
- Bias, fairness, and understanding AI decisions
- AI impacts, governance, and responsibility
- Applying AI in your field and planning next steps
