Two studies measured different parts of the labor market.

Stanford Digital Economy Lab · U.S. payroll employment · workers ages 22–25 · November 2022 to June 2026

  • Employment in occupations classified as highly exposed to AI fell 11%. Employment in less exposed occupations rose 10%. That left the first group about 19% below the comparison path.
  • This compares employment records for two groups of occupations. It does not by itself prove that AI caused the difference.

Federal Reserve Board · U.S. job postings by firms and industries · data through November 2025

  • Firms and industries with higher AI adoption did not reduce their job postings more than the comparison groups.
  • This counts advertised openings. It does not count workers employed or people hired.

Takeaway: Employment records and job postings answer different questions.

A model can score well on one test and still fail other tasks.

A model is trained software that produces an output from an input, such as a written request or a computer screen.

A benchmark is a fixed set of tasks used to compare how well models perform.

SWE-bench Verified · coding test · Stanford AI Index 2026

  • SWE-bench Verified gives a model real software issues and checks whether its proposed fixes pass the software's tests. The reported solve rate rose from 60% to nearly 100% in one year.

OSWorld · computer-use test · Stanford AI Index 2026

  • OSWorld asks a model to complete a fixed set of tasks through a computer interface. Models completed about 66%, so about one task in three still failed.

Takeaway: A benchmark result does not show that a model is reliable on every real task.

Two public predictions about AI come with a date attached.

AGI is a disputed label for AI that matches or exceeds humans across general intellectual work. There is no agreed test for it.

AI could reach AGI as early as 2026.

Elon Musk, in a recorded interview, December 2025 · The date falls inside this course. Before we can judge it, we have to agree on what test AGI would have to pass.

Within one to five years, about half of entry-level white-collar jobs are eliminated and unemployment reaches 10–20%.

Dario Amodei, in an interview published by Axios, May 2025 · The five-year window runs to May 2030, so it is still open.

Where the number stands today: U.S. unemployment was 4.1% in July 2026, as reported by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Takeaway: Both windows are still open, so the July 2026 unemployment rate cannot yet confirm or refute either prediction.

Where AI helps in my work, and where I still have to check it

Instructor experience · August 2026

Useful now

  • Starting a draft or outline
  • Writing routine code or formatting
  • Sorting material when I can check the result

Not ready to trust on its own

  • Facts or sources I have not checked
  • Decisions that depend on missing context
  • Work I cannot independently evaluate

Takeaway: The useful cases still depend on knowing what to ask and how to check the result.
